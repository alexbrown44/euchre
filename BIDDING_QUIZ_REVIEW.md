# Bidding quiz: questions for review

These are the "Would you call?" questions in the app's Learn mode. Each is a real deal: league-style bots bid until it's your turn, then every choice was scored by dealing the hidden cards a few hundred ways (keeping only deals that fit the calls so far) and playing each choice out. Numbers are **average net points per hand for your team**; score 0-0, game to 10.

A choice counts as right if it is the best, or within 0.05 points a hand of it (or within the measuring error). **Starter** questions have one clear answer, at least 0.4 points ahead of the next choice, and it's the answer the guide's rule of thumb gives.

| | Starter | Seasoned |
|---|---:|---:|
| Questions | 46 | 93 |
| Answer is a call | 28 | 56 |
| Answer is alone | 13 | 23 |
| The guide's rule of thumb gets it wrong | 0 | 20 |

226 spots were simulated; 2 were dropped because the choices were too close to call, and some plain passes were left out so passing doesn't dominate.

If an answer looks wrong for our league, note its number and I'll look at it.

**Where the guide's rule of thumb and the simulator disagree** (worth a look, both for the quiz and for the guide): Q5, Q9, Q38, Q39, Q43, Q46, Q48, Q49, Q50, Q54, Q55, Q58, Q60, Q61, Q65, Q69, Q82, Q86, Q90, Q91.
